Ho installato ReShade con il nome [I]d3d11.dll[/I] invece del solito [I]dxgi.dll[/I]. [SIZE=4][B]What is not in it, and why[/B][/SIZE]
The mod files are not included and never will be: they contain NVIDIA-derived data and the original projects' licences forbid redistributing them. The installer builds the runtime on your PC from Blanco's official setup, downloaded from his GitHub page and checked against its fingerprints. The weights, if you do not have them yet, are downloaded by the add-on's official installer through its own channel.

[SPOILER="How I got it working with sider (for tinkerers)"]
I installed ReShade under the name [I]d3d11.dll[/I] instead of the usual [I]dxgi.dll[/I]. The reasoning: sider hooks CreateDXGIFactory1, so I preferred not to let ReShade use the same hook point. With d3d11.dll, on my install, the two coexist with no trouble at all: I checked both logs, with all my patches active.
I did not test the dxgi.dll route thoroughly, so I would not rule out that it works on other setups. If you try it, post your result in the thread.

[B]Technical note:[/B] on PES 2021 the add-on sees the game's depth but it is unusable (panel: "Depth: ... binds seen, NONE USABLE", measured "depth 0.0000..0.0156"). Older engines do not fill the depth range: the network works on the final image and estimates the rest, even on DirectX 11.
[/SPOILER]
